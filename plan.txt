The "Neural Kernel" (OS for Autonomous Agents)
Instead of just a language like MOL, he could build a micro-kernel designed specifically to host AI agents.
The Concept: Traditional Operating Systems (Windows/Linux) are built for humans. A "Neural Kernel" would be built for agents, where every system call is a prompt and every file is a memory vector.
Why it makes him unique: It moves him from "AI developer" to "System Architect." If he creates the environment where agents actually live and breathe safely, he becomes the provider of the "Windows" of the AI era.
Stack: C++, Rust, and a tight integration with MOL for self-tracing.
